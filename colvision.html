About Colvision (Pty) Ltd

Engineering Intelligent, Secure & Connected Environments Since 2006

From humble beginnings in the heart of Gaborone's International Commerce Park in May 2006, Colvision (Pty) Ltd has grown into one of Botswana's most trusted names in security systems, electronics, power backup, and telecommunication solutions.

Headquartered at Plot 2074, Block 3 Industrial (PO Box 411403 Metsef Gaborone), our central workshop serves as the nerve center for diagnostics, repairs, planning, and operational coordination—supported by dedicated on-site project offices at major client deployments.

Fully registered with CIPA and BURS, Colvision guarantees regulatory compliance, technical excellence, and local empowerment across homes, farms, schools, hospitals, retail chains, and government institutions.

Risk & Continuity Management

Resilient Engineering & Supply Chain Continuity

How Colvision protects client infrastructure against electrical surges, hardware obsolescence, and supply delays.

Power fluctuations pose a critical risk to sensitive electronics. Colvision integrates industrial surge protectors, online UPS units (2–60 kVA), and automatic voltage regulators directly into every project architecture to safeguard client investments.
To eliminate local stockouts and specialized component delays, we maintain vetted direct supply partnerships in South Africa and China—ensuring rapid procurement, authentic OEM warranties, and bulk purchasing cost savings for our clients.
We overcome technology adoption barriers through live system demonstrations and tailored consultations, backed by continuous technical staff training and binding Service Level Agreements (SLAs) with guaranteed response times.
